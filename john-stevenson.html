From requirement to onboarding

One recruiter handles every stage, so you get one point of contact and fewer dropped handoffs.

  1. Requirement discovery

    We align on the job profile, client constraints and bill rate before anything is submitted.

  2. Bench sourcing

    I market pre-qualified consultants and US Master's graduates against your requirement.

  3. Screening and prep

    Each candidate is checked for technical depth, communication and visa validity, then prepared for the client.

  4. Submission and interview rounds

    Profiles go to the client quickly, and I manage scheduling and feedback through every round.

  5. Rate closure and onboarding

    We finalize the C2C contract, complete onboarding clearances and stay in touch with the consultant after start.
